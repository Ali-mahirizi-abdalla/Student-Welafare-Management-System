About Campus Care.

University student welfare management and administrative services.

Campus Care is the central web portal for university student welfare services. It links students with hall wardens, academic coordinators, health staff, and finance officers.

Students can complete room bookings, submit deferments, schedule clinic visits, and clear payments directly from any smartphone or computer without queuing for physical forms.
